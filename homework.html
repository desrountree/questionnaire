01 Before we start

This is where you tell me about your business in your own words. There’s no wrong answer, and you don’t need to know anything about design.

  • It takes about 10 minutes.
  • Your answers save as you go, so you can stop and come back on the same device.
  • If more than one person has a say on the logo, each of you does your own noun list at the end.

When you’re done, hit Send and it drops into my inbox.

03 Brand nouns

This is the most useful thing you can give me.

A noun is a person, place or thing. I’m after a list of nouns connected to your business: what you sell, where you are, how you work, what you love about it.

The test is simple: could you draw it? “Trust” is hard to draw. A key, a shield or a lock isn’t. When a word like trust comes to mind, try to name an object that carries its meaning.

For a bakery, a list might start: rolling pin, wheat sheaf, oven, delivery bike, the old clock on the wall.

Obvious ones are welcome, because some of the best logos combine two ordinary things. Aim for about 10. More is great.
